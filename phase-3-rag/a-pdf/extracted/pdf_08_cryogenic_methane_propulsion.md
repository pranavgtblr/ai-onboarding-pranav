Liquid Methane (CH4) & Liquid Oxygen (LOX) Densification Protocol
1. Propellant Subcooling
Liquid methane is densified to 90 K and liquid oxygen to 66 K using closed-cycle
neon cryocoolers, increasing bulk propellant loading density by 8.4 percent inside
the Earth Return Vehicle tanks.
2. Boil-Off Zero-Vent Storage
Cryogenic tanks incorporate 40-layer double-aluminized Mylar multi-layer insulation
(MLI) and pulse-tube active re-liquefaction to eliminate venting losses over
500-sol dwell periods.
3. Transfer Line Purge
All cryogenic propellant transfer lines are vacuum-jacketed and pre-chilled with
gaseous helium before liquid propellant transit to prevent cavitation shockwaves
in turbopumps.
