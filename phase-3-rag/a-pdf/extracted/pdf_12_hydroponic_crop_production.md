Hydroponic & Aeroponic Biomass Production Guidelines
1. Automated Nutrient Film Technique
Continuous flow nutrient channels support dwarf wheat, sweet potato, and leafy
greens under dual-spectrum red/blue LED arrays operating at 350 micromoles/m2/s.
2. Atmosphere Composition & Enrichment
Greenhouse modules maintain carbon dioxide enrichment at 1200 ppm and 65 percent
relative humidity, accelerating crop vegetative growth cycles by 38 percent compared
to terrestrial baselines.
3. Biological Waste Recirculation
Composted inedible biomass feeds anaerobic digestors, yielding bio-available
nitrogen and methane while spirulina algae vats capture secondary carbon dioxide.
