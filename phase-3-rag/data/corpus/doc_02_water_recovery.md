# Closed-Loop Water Recovery & Vapor Compression Distillation

## 1. Architectural Architecture
The Water Recovery System (WRS) at Odyssey Base achieves a 98.2% closed-loop recycling efficiency. The system processes crew perspiration, cabin humidity condensate, hygiene wastewater, and urine. Water is collected into greywater storage sumps before passing through multi-stage filtration.

## 2. Vapor Compression Distillation (VCD)
Urine processing uses a rotary drum Vapor Compression Distillation centrifuge operating at 40 rpm. The rotational centrifugal field simulates 1.2g of artificial gravity, ensuring liquid-vapor separation in Mars's 0.38g gravity field. Acid pretreatment involves Chromium Trioxide and Sulfuric Acid at a pH of 1.8 to prevent microbial breakdown of urea into volatile ammonia.

## 3. Catalytic Oxidation Reactor & Mineralization
Distillate passes through a catalytic oxidation reactor heated to 135°C at 3.5 bar pressure in the presence of platinum-iridium catalysts, oxidizing remaining volatile organic carbons (VOCs). Post-reactor mineralization injects 25 ppm calcium carbonate and 15 ppm magnesium sulfate to ensure palatable, non-corrosive drinking water. Electrical conductivity must remain below 1.5 microSiemens/cm.

## 4. Daily Allocations and Reserves
Crew members are allocated 15 liters of recycled water per sol (3L drinking, 4L food prep, 8L hygiene). Subsurface ice mining wells at Arcadia Planitia supply 120 liters of fresh makeup water weekly to offset the 1.8% system loss.
