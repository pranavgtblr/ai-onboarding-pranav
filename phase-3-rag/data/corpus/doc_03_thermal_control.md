# Habitation Active Thermal Control System (ATCS)

## 1. Temperature Regimes & Thermal Load
Mars surface temperatures range from -125°C during polar winter nights to +20°C at equatorial midday. Odyssey Base maintains internal pressurized habitat modules at a steady 21.5°C +/- 1.0°C with 45% relative humidity. Total internal heat dissipation from avionics, reactors, science racks, and human metabolism averages 48 kW.

## 2. Internal & External Fluid Loops
The ATCS consists of two distinct thermal loops:
- Internal Heat Collection Loop: Circulates food-grade uninhibited water-glycol (70/30 mix) through cold plates.
- External Heat Rejection Loop: Circulates low-viscosity silicone fluid (polydimethylsiloxane) capable of flowing without freezing down to -140°C.
Heat is transferred between the two loops via three titanium counter-flow plate heat exchangers (HX-101, 102, 103).

## 3. Deployable Radiator Panels
External heat rejection utilizes eight dual-sided deployable composite radiators with silvered Teflon coatings. Total radiation surface area is 320 square meters. The radiators emit in the 8-14 micrometer infrared atmospheric window.

## 4. Cold Night Survival Mode
During winter solstice nights when external temps drop below -110°C, bypass valves reduce radiator circulation by 85% to avoid over-cooling. Auxiliary electric strip heaters (12 kW total) powered by the Kilopower reactor maintain battery bay temperatures above +5°C.
