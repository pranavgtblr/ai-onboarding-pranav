# Kilopower KRUSTY-II 40 kWe Fission Reactor Operations

## 1. Reactor Core Specifications
Primary electrical baseload for Odyssey Base is supplied by two Kilopower KRUSTY-II nuclear fission units. Each unit utilizes an enriched Uranium-235 (U-235) molybdenum alloy solid metal core cast into a single cylinder. The core thermal output is 160 kWth, producing 40 kWe net electrical power via closed-loop Stirling power conversion engines. The reactor has an operational design lifespan of 15 terrestrial years without refueling.

## 2. Heat Pipe Sodium Transport
Core heat is transferred to the Stirling convertors using sodium heat pipes operating at 800°C. Liquid sodium vaporizes at the reactor core evaporator section and condenses at the Stirling engine heater heads. Capillary wick structures return liquid sodium to the core without requiring any mechanical pumps.

## 3. Stirling Conversion Engines
Each reactor utilizes eight dual-opposed Stirling engines (model ST-5000). The engines employ helium gas at 60 bar charge pressure as the working fluid. Linear alternators convert reciprocating piston movement directly into 400V 3-phase AC power at 400 Hz, with a thermal-to-electric conversion efficiency of 28.5%.

## 4. Radiation Safety & Burial Zone
The reactors are emplaced in a natural volcanic crater 1.2 kilometers north-west of the main habitat. The core is subterranean, shielded by 3 meters of compacted Martian regolith and an annular boron-carbide / lithium-hydride neutron shadow shield. Radiation dose rate at the habitat perimeter is less than 0.05 microSieverts per hour.
