# Photovoltaic Solar Farm & Dust Mitigation Protocols

## 1. Solar Farm Architecture
Odyssey Base maintains a 1.5-hectare solar field comprising 480 bifacial InGaP/InGaAs/Ge triple-junction solar panels. At solar noon under clear skies (optical depth tau = 0.3), solar irradiance at Mars's orbital distance is ~590 W/m2. The solar array produces a peak power of 65 kW, which recharges the base energy storage reserves and operates the day-shift ISRU methane production plant.

## 2. Electrostatic Dust Removal System (EDS)
Fine ferric oxide (Fe2O3) dust particles accumulate on panel surfaces at a rate of 0.28% surface obscuration daily. The panels are equipped with embedded indium-tin-oxide (ITO) transparent electrode tracks. Every sol at 03:00 local time, a four-phase travelling electrostatic wave (1,500 Volts at 20 Hz) is energized for 8 minutes, repelling and lofting charged dust particles into the wind with a 94% clearance efficiency.

## 3. Global Dust Storm Emergency Procedure (Tau > 3.0)
When atmospheric optical depth (tau) exceeds 3.0 during planet-encircling dust storms, solar output collapses to < 8% of nominal. Under Storm Condition Amber, non-essential experiments are shut down, greenhouse lighting is cut by 60%, and power is drawn exclusively from the dual Kilopower nuclear reactors.

## 4. Mechanical Wiper Servicing
Automated rover units (Scout-1 and Scout-2) carry rotating microfiber wiper attachments to clean sensor calibration targets and optical lenses every 14 sols.
