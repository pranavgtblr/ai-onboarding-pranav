# Lithium-Sulfur (Li-S) Energy Storage Subsystem

## 1. Battery Bank Chemistry & Energy Density
The habitat energy storage buffer consists of 4 modular Lithium-Sulfur (Li-S) battery banks with a combined capacity of 450 kWh. Li-S was selected over conventional Li-ion due to its superior gravimetric energy density (420 Wh/kg vs 250 Wh/kg). The cells operate at nominal 2.15V with a solid-state garnet-type ceramic electrolyte (LLZO) that eliminates thermal runaway risk.

## 2. Thermal Management of Cells
Li-S chemistry exhibits elevated internal resistance at sub-zero temperatures. The battery compartment is maintained at +22°C +/- 2°C using waste thermal energy from the internal water-glycol cooling loop. If temperature drops below +10°C, maximum discharge rate is automatically throttled by 40%.

## 3. Depth of Discharge (DoD) & Cycle Life
Nominal daily depth-of-discharge is capped at 65% to guarantee a minimum lifespan of 3,500 Martian sol cycles. State of Charge (SoC) balance is managed across 1,200 individual cell channels by an active inductive balancing BMS.

## 4. Emergency Black-Start Protocol
A dedicated isolated 25 kWh emergency black-start battery (Bank Epsilon) is reserved strictly to restart reactor cooling pumps and control rod actuators in the event of a total main bus collapse.
