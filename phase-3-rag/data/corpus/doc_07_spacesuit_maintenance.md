# Extravehicular Mobility Unit (EMU-X) Servicing Manual

## 1. Suit Architecture & Pressure Profile
The EMU-X is a hybrid hard-upper-torso (HUT) planetary spacesuit designed for Martian terrain operations. Operating pressure is maintained at 29.6 kPa (4.3 psi) using 100% oxygen. This low-pressure regime eliminates the risk of joint ballooning while providing sufficient O2 partial pressure to prevent hypoxia.

## 2. Dust Sealing & Magnetic Joint Bearings
Martian dust grains are sharp, jagged, and electrostatically charged. Suit rotary bearings in the shoulders, wrists, hips, and ankles utilize hermetically sealed ferrofluid magnetic seals and dual Viton O-rings to prevent dust intrusion.

## 3. Portable Life Support System (PLSS)
The PLSS backpack provides 8 hours of nominal EVA duration, with a 30-minute secondary emergency reserve. Carbon dioxide removal is handled by twin regenerable amine beds (Metal-Organic Framework MOF-74), which desorb CO2 into the vacuum of space every 20 minutes without consuming consumables.

## 4. Pre-Breathe Protocol & Decompression Sickness
Crew members must undergo a 90-minute in-suit pre-breathe protocol breathing pure O2 while exercising at light resistance before suit ingress, washing out dissolved nitrogen to lower the tissue R-value below 1.2.
