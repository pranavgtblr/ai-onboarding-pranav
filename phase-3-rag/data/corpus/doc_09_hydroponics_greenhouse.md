# Aeroponic Greenhouse & Botanical Sustenance Facility

## 1. Greenhouse Architecture
The greenhouse module (Bio-Dome Beta) spans 220 square meters of vertical aeroponic cultivation racks. Plants grow suspended in air, with their roots intermittently misted every 90 seconds with a nutrient solution containing balanced potassium, nitrates, phosphorus, and iron chelates.

## 2. Core Food Crops
The diet of the crew is 35% supplemented by fresh produce from five staple crops:
- Dwarf Sweet Potatoes (high caloric density, beta-carotene)
- Micro-Tom Tomatoes (rapid 60-day seed-to-fruit cycle, lycopene)
- Soybeans (crude protein and lipid production)
- Radishes & Leafy Greens (vitamin C, high harvest index)
- Spirulina Algae Bioreactors (complete amino acid profile, 65% protein by dry weight)

## 3. Lighting Spectrum & Photoperiods
Lighting is delivered by tailored red (660 nm), deep blue (450 nm), and far-red (730 nm) LED arrays delivering a photosynthetic photon flux density (PPFD) of 600 micromoles/m2/s. Photoperiod is maintained at 18 hours light / 6 hours dark.

## 4. Atmospheric Composition & CO2 Enrichment
Greenhouse CO2 concentration is artificially elevated to 1,200 ppm (compared to 400 ppm on Earth), boosting photosynthetic rates by 42% and drastically reducing transpiration water loss.
