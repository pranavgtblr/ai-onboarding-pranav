# Emergency Medical Protocols & Radiation Sickness Management

## 1. Acute Radiation Syndrome (ARS) Diagnostic Protocol
During major Solar Particle Events (SPEs), unshielded crew members are at risk of Acute Radiation Syndrome. Base medical bays use active biodosimetry kits measuring chromosomal dicentric aberrations in peripheral blood lymphocytes. Exposure thresholds:
- Mild (0.5 to 1.5 Gy): Nausea, mild fatigue, transient drop in white blood cell count.
- Moderate (1.5 to 4.0 Gy): Severe vomiting within 4 hours, thrombocytopenia, infection vulnerability.
- Severe (> 4.0 Gy): Bone marrow suppression, gastrointestinal syndrome, requires intensive hospital care.

## 2. Treatment & Pharmacopeia
Patients with exposures > 1.0 Gy receive Granulocyte Colony-Stimulating Factor (G-CSF, Filgrastim) at 5 mcg/kg daily to stimulate neutrophil regeneration. Prophylactic broad-spectrum antibiotics (Ciprofloxacin and Vancomycin) and anti-emetics (Ondansetron 8mg IV) are administered immediately.

## 3. Surgical Facility & Negative-Pressure Isolation
The habitat medical bay contains a dual-purpose surgical theater equipped with an automated surgical robot (DaVinci-Mars), closed-loop anesthesia delivery using recycled xenon, and negative-pressure isolation airlocks to prevent biohazard spread.

## 4. Decompression Sickness (Bends) Treatment
Chamber 2 of the main airlock can function as a multi-place hyperbaric treatment chamber pressurized up to 2.8 bar breathing 100% oxygen under US Navy Treatment Table 6 protocols for air embolism or decompression sickness.
