# Cryogenic Fuel Storage & Zero-Boil-Off (ZBO) Systems

## 1. Propellant Inventory
Odyssey Base stores 85 metric tons of liquid methane (LCH4, boiling point -161.6°C at 1 atm) and 340 metric tons of liquid oxygen (LOX, boiling point -182.96°C) for the Earth Return Vehicle (ERV). Propellants are stored in double-walled vacuum-jacketed spherical aluminum-lithium tanks (Tank Farm Alpha).

## 2. Multi-Layer Insulation (MLI) & Vacuum Annulus
The tanks feature an outer stainless steel vacuum shell with an evacuated annulus pressure < 10^-4 Torr. Within the vacuum gap, 60 alternating layers of double-aluminized Mylar and Dacron scrim insulation reflect 99.2% of ambient radiant heat.

## 3. Active Cryocoolers (Pulse Tube)
To achieve Zero-Boil-Off (ZBO) over 500-sol holding periods, the tanks are cooled by active closed-cycle Pulse Tube Cryocoolers (PTCs) delivering 40W of heat lift at 90 K. The cryocoolers run on helium gas and consume 3.2 kW of electrical power per tank.

## 4. Tank Ullage Pressure Management
Tank internal pressure is maintained between 1.8 bar and 2.4 bar. If cryocooler failure occurs and tank pressure reaches 3.2 bar, automated pneumatic relief valves vent boiled-off gaseous methane through catalytic burners, oxidizing it into inert CO2 and H2O to prevent combustible vapor clouds on the pad.
