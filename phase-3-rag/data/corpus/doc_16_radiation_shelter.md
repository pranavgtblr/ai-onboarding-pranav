# Solar Particle Event (SPE) Radiation Storm Shelter Protocols

## 1. Solar Storm Early Warning System
Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs) directed toward Mars are detected by the Sun-monitoring Deep Space Weather Probe (Helios-4) in solar orbit. Depending on solar wind velocity (typically 400 to 1,200 km/s), base personnel have between 18 and 36 hours of advance warning before the high-energy proton shockfront strikes the Martian magnetosphere.

## 2. The Bunker Citadel (Storm Shelter)
The central storm shelter (The Citadel) is excavated 6 meters below the natural surface beneath a basalt volcanic ridge. The ceiling and walls are lined with a 40 cm water jacket (incorporating the base's 45,000-liter potable water reserve) and 15 cm of high-density polyethylene (HDPE). Hydrogen-rich materials are exceptionally effective at absorbing and slowing fast solar protons without generating secondary Bremsstrahlung radiation.

## 3. Alarm Stages & Muster Protocols
- Alert Yellow (SPE Warning issued by NOAA Space Weather): All surface EVA operations canceled; rovers return to base.
- Alert Orange (Proton flux > 100 pfu at > 10 MeV): All crew members ingress habitat; airlocks locked down.
- Alert Red (Shockfront arrival imminent): All 12 crew members must muster in the Citadel within 15 minutes.

## 4. Bunker Life Support Capabilities
The Citadel has independent air scrubbers, food rations, and medical bays capable of sustaining the entire crew for up to 14 consecutive sols during extended coronal storm events.
