# Chronology of Base Incidents & Safety Lessons (2078-2088)

## 1. Incident 2079-Sol-142: Thermal Loop Depressurization
A micro-meteoroid penetrated the unarmored return line of Radiator Bank 3. The silicone coolant loop lost 35% of its fluid inventory before automated isolation valves engaged. Lesson: All external fluid lines were retrofitted with Kevlar-reinforced Whipple shields.

## 2. Incident 2082-Sol-088: Dust Storm Reactor Scram
During a planet-wide dust storm, severe electrostatic discharge (arching across 8 kV differential) damaged the digital control bus on Kilopower Reactor 1, causing an automated safety scram. The base ran on emergency batteries for 18 hours. Lesson: Installed fiber-optic isolated control busses and grounding rods sunk 15 meters into subsurface salty permafrost.

## 3. Incident 2085-Sol-310: Sabatier Reactor Thermal Runaway
A faulty thermocouple on the Sabatier methanation catalyst bed reported 250°C when the true temperature reached 580°C. The catalyst sintered, reducing methane yield by 60%. Lesson: Mandated triple-redundant voted thermocouples on all ISRU reactors.

## 4. Incident 2087-Sol-204: Rover-2 Wheel Hub Seizure
Fine electrostatic dust breached the tertiary labyrinth seal on wheel #4 of Rover Ares-II during an expedition to Jezero Crater. The crew was stranded 140 km from base and successfully towed back by Rover Ares-I. Lesson: Upgraded all wheel hubs to magnetic ferrofluid hermetic seals.
