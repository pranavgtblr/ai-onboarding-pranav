# Protocol Omega: Catastrophic Base Evacuation & Abort Order

## 1. Definition & Activation Criteria
Protocol Omega is the irreversible executive emergency procedure governing complete abandonment of the surface habitat and immediate emergency launch of the Earth Return Vehicle (ERV). It was established in 2088 following the Phobos-Deimos Safety Review. It can be declared ONLY by unanimous concurrence of the Base Commander and the Mission Flight Surgeon, or by direct override command from Earth Mission Control.

## 2. Triggers for Protocol Omega
1. Unrecoverable atmospheric hull breach exceeding 0.5 m2 equivalent orifice area across multiple modules.
2. Total loss of reactor primary coolant combined with secondary battery depletion below 15%.
3. Irreversible biological contamination of the habitat ecosystem by foreign toxicity or virulent pathogen.
4. Severe seismic rupture of the underlying Arcadia Planitia lava tube structure.

## 3. Evacuation Timeline (T-0 to Launch)
- T-00:00: Siren sounds; automated telemetry locks habitat blast doors.
- T-00:15: Crew musters in the ERV boarding gantry in pressure suits.
- T-00:30: ERV life support activated; umbilical purge complete.
- T-01:00: Methane/LOX rocket ignition and liftoff to low Mars orbit (LMO).

## 4. Post-Evacuation Base Safing
Before liftoff, automated routines safe the nuclear reactors (dropping boron-carbide control rods into the core) and depressurize all surface fuel lines to prevent post-departure explosions.
