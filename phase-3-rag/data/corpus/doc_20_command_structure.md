# Mission Command Structure, Crew Roles & Authority Matrix

## 1. Chain of Command
Odyssey Base operates under a structured military-civilian hybrid chain of command:
1. Base Commander (CDR): Final operational authority on all surface activities, EVA authorization, and resource prioritization.
2. Executive Officer (XO): Manages day-to-day station maintenance, schedule deconfliction, and internal habitat logistics.
3. Chief Medical Officer / Flight Surgeon (CMO): Absolute medical veto authority over crew duty assignments, sleep cycles, and EVA fitness.
4. Lead Systems Engineer (LSE): Direct authority over reactor power distribution, ECLSS balancing, and ISRU operations.
5. Science Operations Lead (SCI): Manages laboratory allocation, sample return integrity, and rover science targets.

## 2. Operational Autonomy vs Earth Control
Due to communication latency (6 to 45 minutes round-trip), Odyssey Base operates with a 'Tactical Surface Autonomy' doctrine. Real-time decisions regarding immediate crew safety, rover navigation, and life support anomalies are made locally by the CDR. Strategic decisions (trajectory burns, mission timeline extensions, primary reactor shutdown) require formal Flight Director authorization from Johnson Space Center (JSC) Mission Control.

## 3. Handover & Shift Protocols
The 12-person crew is partitioned into two 6-person staggered watches (Alpha Shift: 06:00 to 18:00 Mars Local Time; Beta Shift: 18:00 to 06:00 Mars Local Time). Shift handover involves a mandatory 30-minute structured briefing reviewing power bus stability, tank ullage pressures, greenhouse moisture levels, and atmospheric scrub rates.
