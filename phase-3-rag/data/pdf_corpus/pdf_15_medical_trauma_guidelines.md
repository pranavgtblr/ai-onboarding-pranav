Extraterrestrial Medical Emergency & Hypobaric Decompression Guidelines
1. Acute Decompression Sickness (DCS)
DCS protocols require immediate crew placement into the 2.8 ATA hyperbaric treatment
lock, administering 100 percent humidified normothermic oxygen for a 120-minute
cycle.
2. Microgravity Fracture Stabilization
Long-bone fractures are stabilized with lightweight carbon-fiber external fixator
splints and ultrasound bone-growth stimulation to counter microgravity osteopenia.
3. Telemedicine Diagnostic Ultrasound
Autonomous ultrasound scanning with real-time Earth surgical teleconsultation
guides thoracic cavity needle decompressions in suspected pneumothorax incidents.
